Kestler Ads: Amazon advertising for brand owners

We are an Amazon PPC agency based in Hannover, Germany. We help brand owners in Europe grow profitably on Amazon with data-driven Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display campaigns.

What we do

  • PPC management: campaign structure, keyword research, bid and budget management, negative keywords, weekly optimisation.
  • Search query analysis: monthly Search Query Performance and search term analysis to find the queries where a brand wins or loses share.
  • Listing optimisation: titles, bullet points and backend keywords based on real search data.
  • Reporting: a clear monthly report on sales, advertising cost, TACoS and organic ranking for each client.

How we work with your Amazon data

With your permission we connect to your Seller Central account through Amazon's official Selling Partner API. You authorize our application once in Seller Central and can revoke the access at any time under Settings, User permissions (Apps and services).

  • We only read the reports we need for your account: search query and Brand Analytics data, sales and traffic, inventory, listings and prices.
  • We do not access buyer personal data such as names, addresses or phone numbers.
  • Your data is used only for the work we do for you and is never shared with other clients.
  • Data is stored encrypted with restricted access. See our Privacy Policy.

For campaign management we use Amazon's official Amazon Ads API. You or your team grant our application access to your advertising account once through Login with Amazon and can remove the access at any time. Through the Ads API we manage the Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display campaigns you have asked us to manage, and read their performance reports. Every change to your campaigns follows the plan agreed with you.

Our application: Kestler Ads Reporting

Kestler Ads Reporting is the application our clients authorize in Seller Central. It is only available to Kestler Ads clients.

  • Downloads the client's Search Query Performance and search term reports every month.
  • Combines them with sales, traffic, inventory, listing and price data of the same account.
  • Shows which search queries bring impressions, clicks and sales, and where advertising should change.
  • Produces the monthly PPC and SEO report we send to the client.
  • Manages Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display campaigns through the Amazon Ads API, for clients who have authorized it.
